# Tesla Preset Guidelines (`/medesign tesla`)

## Archetype Mapping
- **Primary Archetype:** Archetype 18 (Industrial / Hardware Product)
- **Secondary Archetype:** Archetype 32 (GIS Situation Awareness & Telemetry)

## Invariants & Design Principles
1. **Brutal Geometry & Minimal Radii:**
   Outer card radius `4px`, inner nested radius `2px`. Avoid large bubbly curves; adhere to razor-sharp automotive engineering surfaces.
2. **Telemetric Typography:**
   - Display/Headlines: Space Grotesk / Gotham with crisp technical presence.
   - Values & Metrics: Monospace (`IBM Plex Mono` / `JetBrains Mono`) with `tabular-nums` for real-time telemetry readouts.
   - Letter-spacing: `+0.08em` uppercase tracking on status badges and controls.
3. **High-Velocity Precision Motion:**
   `precision` motion profile (100ms entry, 70ms exit). State feedback must feel immediate, like physical vehicle switches.
4. **Contrast & Night Mode Invariant:**
   Deep obsidian background `#050505`, crisp white metrics `#ffffff`, Tesla Crimson accent `oklch(0.58 0.24 28)` for primary action and critical warnings.
